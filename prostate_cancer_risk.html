How to Read This Chart

Each bar shows the estimated lifetime diagnosis risk as risk factors are layered. These are conservative estimates based on published relative risks from SEER, VA, and NIH studies. Because these risk factors partially overlap (e.g., Black veterans are overrepresented in VA data), the compound figure is not a simple multiplication — it reflects adjusted, published estimates for co-occurring risk profiles. Individual risk varies; always consult a physician.
